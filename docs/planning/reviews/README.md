# Owner review pages

The things in [TODO.md](../../../TODO.md) under "Waiting on the owner" hold the v1.2.0 tag. These pages turn them into checklists, so you can work through them at your own pace, in the repository, and tick the boxes (`[ ]` becomes `[x]`) as you go. Commit the ticks as you like; nothing depends on the order.

How to use a page:

1. Pick a page below and work through it top to bottom, or take one group of rows.
2. Do what the row says on your control or in the app. A row you cannot answer yet stays unticked.
3. Where something is wrong, write it beside the row (a few words are enough) and leave the box empty.
4. When a review page is fully ticked ([templates](templates.md) and [code data](code-data.md)), a later batch removes the "review pending" marks of those entries. Ticking the box does not change the program by itself.
